How to use the two books

Start in the Practice Book. Every lab has a blue "Why it works" box that links to the Theory Book chapter behind it. Read the theory when the lab makes you curious, not before. Your earlier diode session lives in the Theory Book as T11+. Keep the Reference Shelf (glossary, formulas, symbols) open in a second tab while you build.

Bench practice and real equipment

Construction labs use batteries or isolated extra-low-voltage supplies. The welding track also explains hazardous-voltage equipment: inside-machine work needs verified isolation, stored-energy checks and a competent repair mentor; energised diagnosis needs the model-specific service procedure and an equipped technician. In Australia, installation work and equipment repair have state-specific licensing requirements; T09 explains the distinction. ELV work can also require cabling or security registration.
