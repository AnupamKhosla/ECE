Replay interruptions

The training sequence allows 250 ms to get an acknowledgment. An acknowledgment exactly at 250 ms is accepted when processed before timeout; anything later is held. A lost link after acceptance means disposition is unknown. Reconcile the part position and retained result before assigning another job.

  3. Write a case for both controllers restarting with a part still in the gripper. Specify who confirms its identity, where the evidence is recorded and which new command must wait. Include a stuck-on destination sensor as a separate failure from lost communication.
