## How to compare the evidence

- The earlier Opportunity Radar snapshot in C00 captured 2,764 Australian SEEK ads on 1–2 November. It has keyword-group applicant counts, including a BMS median of 19 across 51 matches. The public current search pages and the Ludhiana employer examples give role and requirement detail.
- A search count depends on query, date, location radius, duplicated ads and role seniority. Review distinct ads that fit your commute and the qualifications stated in your CV. Save the advertisement date and distinguish examples with expired deadlines from vacancies still taking applications.
- The [2026 Jobs and Skills Australia shortage report](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/occupation-shortage) and [Victoria’s recruitment snapshot](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-10/Combined%20-%20Recruitment%20snapshot%20Victoria.pdf) add broad context. For a personal application decision, use the actual job duties, hiring requirements, [Australian work rights](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/working-in-australia/work-rights-and-exploitation/work-restrictions) and travel.
