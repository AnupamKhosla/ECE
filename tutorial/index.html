Australia rule you must know first

In Australia only a licensed electrician may work on mains wiring (anything above 50 V AC or 120 V ripple-free DC). Everything in this course stays at extra-low voltage: batteries, USB, 5 V, 12 V and 24 V DC. The good news: BMS, CCTV, access control, data cabling and telecom work is mostly extra-low voltage, so these are real jobs you can get without an electrical licence.
