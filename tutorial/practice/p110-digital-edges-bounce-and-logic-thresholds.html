P110

See a bouncing switch and clean up a digital edge

Capture a raw switch and a Schmitt-trigger output on the same timebase, then separate electrical filtering from software timing.

Time 45–70 minutesBefore this P07, P15, T15

Build two versions of the same switch input

Use a normally open pushbutton, 10 kΩ pull-up, 1 kΩ series resistor, 1 µF capacitor, 100 nF supply bypass and an SN74HC14 in a documented DIP package. Use a 5 V isolated supply, current limit no higher than 30 mA. Put pin 14 at +5 V and pin 7 at return. Bypass at the IC. Tie unused inputs to return and leave unused outputs open. Pull RAW up through 10 kΩ, and connect the button from RAW to return.

  1. Probe RAW initially without the capacitor, using DC coupling and a falling single trigger around mid-supply. Record the time and number of visible transitions on ten presses and ten releases. A particular switch may produce few bounces.
  2. Wire 1 kΩ from RAW to FILTER, place 1 µF from FILTER to return and connect FILTER to the first inverter input. For a polarised capacitor, its positive end faces FILTER. Capture FILTER and the inverter output together.
  3. The release charges through roughly 11 kΩ, giving a time constant near 11 ms. Holding the button discharges through approximately 1 kΩ, giving about 1 ms. Estimate the threshold reached on each edge using the measured capture and the data sheet. The output changes opposite to the input.

Tektronix shows how to prepare a probe for a clean edge capture.

Explain what the analyser leaves out

A logic analyser decodes a voltage against a threshold and can miss narrow transitions at too low a sample rate. Verify its actual 5 V input tolerance before connecting. The scope shows the analog slope and bounce. A Schmitt input uses different thresholds in the two directions. A software debounce timer adds a separate delay. Decide whether release and press delay are acceptable for your application and rerun the capture after changing one component at a time.

Keep paired raw and filtered captures and label the timebase, trigger point and ground reference. Link the results to P27 · Program a timed controller that can stop for firmware stop logic and P63 · Prove a BMS point from the room to the screen for switch inputs on a building controller.
