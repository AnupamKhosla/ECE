P138

Size a robot drive and trace a startup fault

Select a motor at its loaded operating point. Then use voltage at the source and driver to find out why the controller resets when both wheels start.

Time 50–70 minutesBefore this P13 · T24 · T58

Work backward from the floor

The example chassis and payload total 4 kg. It must accelerate at 0.50 m/s² on a 5° ramp and reach 0.50 m/s. Two driven wheels share the longitudinal force equally. Each wheel has a 50 mm rolling radius and the estimated rolling resistance coefficient is 0.03.

  1. Use 9.81 m/s² for gravity and calculate each force term before adding them. Convert total force to per-wheel torque and calculate wheel rpm.
  2. Apply a 1.5 training margin to wheel torque. With 30:1 reduction and 0.75 efficiency, find the motor’s operating torque at the same vehicle speed.
  3. Compare the synthetic choices below. Keep the motor speed and shaft torque requirements together. Check efficiency, duty and gearbox ratings in a real data sheet.

Check the operating point and pick

2.00 + 3.42 + 1.17 ≈ 6.59 N. At 0.050 m radius this is 0.165 N·m per wheel. With margin, use 0.247 N·m. Wheel speed is 95.5 rpm, so motor speed is about 2,865 rpm and motor torque is about 0.011 N·m. B clears both torque checks. A misses motor torque and C misses the gearbox output requirement.

Locate a loaded voltage drop

For sizing, assume a training driver limits each motor winding to 1.5 A. A simultaneous start at full duty plus 0.25 A electronics load implies about 3.25 A from the source in this simplified case. Compare the source, harness and driver channel ratings at their own terminals and duty. 1.5 A winding current at lower duty does not imply 1.5 A average source current. Use a current-limited isolated bench supply and a secured small motor for a hardware comparison. Use the recorded case below for peak values.

Predict which path fails and calculate its effective resistance during the event. Propose a comparison after inspecting the de-energised connector and wiring. Repeat at the same test load to confirm the repair. For a real trainer, power down before moving connections and use the motor gently within its limits. Measure voltage at both ends of a wire while it carries load.

Check the diagnosis

The measured start-event drop is 1.28 V, of which 1.18 V is in the positive path. That path’s effective resistance is about 0.37 Ω at the observed load. The supply terminals remain close to 6 V. After repair, verify the driver input stays above its documented limit and the controller stays up at the same load. Compare with the Pololu Romi driver and encoder guide for model-specific details.
