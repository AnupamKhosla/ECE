Audit a robot cell trial before signing acceptance

The trial delivers 331 good parts in an hour of planned production. Find out whether both recipes meet the cycle deadline and whether the inspection sample supports a 1% placement-failure limit.

Time 60–85 minutesBefore this T68 · P146

Write the decision rule before opening the results

The pilot covers minute 0 through minute 70 on one elapsed-time clock. Ten scheduled break minutes are outside production. All other time, including changeover and line waits, remains in the planned window. Both recipes must meet the sample cycle limit and the independent placement-evidence limit. The fictional team selected these three gates.

  • At least 320 unique good units per planned production hour for the cell.
  • Nearest-rank P155 cycle duration of at most 9.5 s for each recipe, using all completed attempts.
  • A recorded placement check on each cycle using the exercise limit of 0.50 mm absolute axial offset from the fixture reference. Each recipe needs a one-sided 95% upper binomial bound of at most 1%, conditional on the independent common-rate model.

Find the slow recipe and quantify the evidence gap

Calculate the pooled P155 and each recipe’s P155. For each, retain the serial at the selected rank. Count placement failures and determine the one-sided upper bound from the number actually checked. Then assess whether conditions were sufficiently representative. Recipe A spans two lots. Recipe B has only one lot and comes after one changeover.

Challenge the report and write the remaining acceptance work

Copy the CSV. Change one A placement result to unchecked and compare both the bound and the coverage decision. In another copy remove a production row entirely and find the timing error. For a third trial set one independent placement result to fail. Compare the adjusted bound and investigate the affected serial against the station’s good result. A disagreement deserves a reconciliation before changing the official count.

Draft a retest plan for recipe B with a cycle event trace, representative lots and a known measurement reference. Record the exact rate and placement decision rules, which software and recipe revisions they apply to, who owns the timing or process correction and when acceptance can be reviewed.
