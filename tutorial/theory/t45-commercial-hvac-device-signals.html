What differential pressure measures

Watch. Locate the hood at the diffuser. Compare what it measures with the pressure at the terminal’s pickup. TSI’s Alnor capture hood demonstration.

A two-port instrument reads Δp = phigh − plow. Both sides may be below atmospheric pressure. If upstream is −60 Pa and downstream is −160 Pa relative to the room, the filter difference is +100 Pa. A water column balances the pressure force against a difference in liquid weight. With density ρ, gravitational acceleration g and difference in levels h, Δp = ρgh. At 20 °C, 1 mm of water is about 9.8 Pa.

A filter's pressure drop depends on airflow as well as the filter. For a velocity pressure pickup in a VAV box, airflow is often calculated using a box-specific coefficient and a square-root relationship to differential pressure. Controller units, sign, zero and the pickup geometry all matter. Use the documented coefficient and test procedure for the box. Compare a local pressure observation with the controller input before adjusting. P77 pressure lab · Dwyer Series 605 example · Johnson Controls VAV pickup gain.

Separate actuator command, position and result

With two-position control, the signal selects one of two states. A three-point or floating controller sends open or close while it wants movement and stops driving when it wants the actuator to hold. A controller that estimates position from elapsed travel can be wrong after a power failure or manual movement. A modulating actuator uses a voltage, current or digital setpoint. For 2–10 V direct-acting demand, 50% corresponds to 6 V. Check the model for signal common, direction and whether 0 V has a defined behaviour.

Three different observations answer three different questions. A 6 V reading at the actuator measures demand. The travel indication or feedback reports movement at that device. An independently observed blade, stem, airflow or water response establishes what the mechanism actually achieved. Even full valve travel gives no useful flow when the pump is off or the isolation valve is closed. Loss of signal and loss of supply can cause different responses. Find both in the model sheet and sequence. P78 valve stroke · P79 fan proof · Belimo modulating spring-return example.

Know what a room sensor can tell you

CO₂ concentration in ppm is a fraction by volume. A true CO₂ instrument uses a measurement sensitive to CO₂ absorption. Relative humidity gives water vapour relative to saturation at the measured temperature. A VOC channel and particle sensor measure different quantities. Poor placement, self-heating and incorrect scaling can introduce bias. Polling intervals and data quality determine whether a displayed number is recent enough to use. Agree on an appropriate comparison and calibration procedure before changing any offset. P80 room sensor lab · SCD30 optical CO₂ device example.

Check yourself

A screen requests 75% open. The actuator sees 8 V on its 2–10 V input. Its feedback also reads 8 V, but the valve stem stays in the same position. Which part of the chain did each observation prove? What would you inspect during approved isolation?
