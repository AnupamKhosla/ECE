Choose the work you want to do

Start with ISP technical support if you want a first telecommunications role you can practise with a spare router and a laptop. Start with fibre field trainee or communications apprentice if you want to work with tools, travel between sites and learn under supervision. Data-centre customer operations sits between them. You may patch and test in a rack, then update a ticket or work order. A mobile network role can be customer-side activation and coverage support, desk-based fault response, or equipment installation and test.

Watch a Melbourne-relevant field role in practice. Look for customer contact, site work and the tests a technician has to explain. Meet Jacob, a field technician for the nbn network.

RouteDo these in orderEvidence to show

Before buying tools or booking training, take two ads you would actually apply for and mark the required qualifications, work rights, roster, driving, travel, physically demanding tasks and tools supplied. Pick one case you can explain in two minutes. Apply to field intakes and entry or support roles that fit those conditions. Use synthetic labels and no customer details in the portfolio record.

Check what each credential allows

Telephone, desk and network monitoring roles have requirements set by the employer. Regulated customer cabling has a separate ACMA framework. When an unregistered person performs customer cabling work, a cabler registered for that scope must directly supervise them at all times. Some specialist work needs extra competencies. Carrier-side work has its own authorisation and network-specific training. Ask the employer or training provider which pathway applies to their actual job.
