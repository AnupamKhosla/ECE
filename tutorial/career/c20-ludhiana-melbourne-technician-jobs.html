Which route should you try first?

In Ludhiana, apply for industrial maintenance, instrumentation support and machine field-service work. Those jobs can build the same meter, sensor and controller skills you need for a textile-electronics repair service. Try the hosiery repair pilot separately so you can compare real shop work with employment. C19 Ludhiana shop research.

In Melbourne, start with junior bench repair, assembly and equipment service roles. Look for positions that train you on specific equipment. Keep BMS as a second search for trainee, junior and supervised controls roles. A full BMS technician job usually involves diagnosing sensors, controller points, communications and HVAC responses. If your priority is physical installation, check trade assistant and formal apprenticeship roles as a distinct route.

Check what the Melbourne employer can put you on

Ask who supervises the work, which tasks are covered on day one and which qualifications or registration the employer needs you to hold. Victoria has a specific plumbing class for mechanical services restricted to duct fixing. Refrigerant handling has separate Australian Refrigeration Council licence categories, including one for trainees working under supervision. Victorian fixed electrical work and disconnect/reconnect have their own licensing and apprentice rules. The actual duties and training arrangement determine the route. Confirm work rights and any training contract before paying for training. Build two interview examples that work in both cities

  1. One bench repair. Use a known-good reference and an intermittent connector or sensor fault. Record the symptom, reference and suspect readings, your diagnosis and the final repeat test. Photos should show the connector or test point. Use P58 for the worksheet or P69 for a Ludhiana-specific case.

  2. One field-controls job. Mount and label the sensor in P54, then prove the room, cable, terminal and screen tag in P63. For a factory maintenance role, substitute P39 and show which input or feedback caused the stop.

  3. Update the job list weekly. Capture the ad, suburb, required experience, whether training is offered, licence or visa limits and application deadline. Merge reposts for the same opening. Apply when the duties and essential requirements fit. Use the next study session to fill a gap that repeats in several ads.
