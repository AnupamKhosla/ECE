Choose a path into controls, electronics test or automation

Building controls is one good route into hands-on technical work. Compare it with electronics test, industrial maintenance, field service and the longer path into robot integration.

Read the requirements behind the title

  • Transition from HVAC into BMS. The Johnson Controls Sydney trainee listing asks for an HVAC qualification and commercial HVAC service experience. Its training is in building controls. Johnson Controls trainee role · Experienced service role.
  • Learn building controls through an electrical apprenticeship. Integrata advertised for first- or second-year apprentices in Sydney. Tasks include cable tray and wiring work alongside supervised testing and commissioning. The listing asks for a current White Card and spells out the TAFE training and work eligibility. Alerton also lists an electrical apprentice role alongside installer and service roles. Integrata electrical apprentice · Alerton role descriptions.
  • Start with bench assembly and test. A Science Squad advert in Tuggerah sought an adult trainee to assemble and test scientific instruments and robot arms under supervision. The ad describes drawings, test checklists and quality records. That makes a documented board retest or robot fault log relevant to the work. Science Squad assembly and test advert.
  • Train in warehouse automation. Dematic’s Australian programme takes applicants for electrical and mechanical trade apprenticeships. Its programme describes progression into equipment maintenance and field service. Dematic Australia apprenticeships.

Build evidence over four weeks

  1. Week 1. Set up the bench and photograph a measured voltage and continuity check. Create a one-page record with labelled probes, expected readings and measured results. Do the first P22 site task. Identify two beginner titles in your chosen city.
  2. Week 2. For controls do P37 and P38, for repair do P26 and one documented module in P34 or P36, or for electronics test do P48 and P51. Save one annotated setup and a meaningful pass/fail measurement.
  3. Week 3. Do P39 and P40 for automation and service, or P42 and P43 if robots are closer to the advertised work. Reproduce a harmless fault and prove recovery. Keep the evidence in a portfolio case sheet.
  4. Week 4. Read at least five ads from each of two role families. Put required and preferred criteria in separate columns, plus training, shifts, travel and tasks. Apply to roles where you meet stated requirements or the employer advertises a path to complete them. Add a longer-term technical project such as P46 and P47 after you have one repair or commissioning case you can explain.

Keep your legal work scope and work eligibility separate from what you have practised. Ask the relevant employer or authority about the role’s supervised and licensed tasks. C11 · Licences and training routes · C16 · Build a portfolio case.

Translate portfolio evidence into an interview answer

Give the reported fault, one plausible cause you ruled out, the actual evidence that led to the fix and the closing test. Example: a PLC lamp never proved the branch ran. The proof input had remained false for 3 s with healthy communications. A corrected trainer input restored proof and passed the same start, stop and timeout checklist. Say what you did under simulation or supervision.
