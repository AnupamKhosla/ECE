## What shaped the training

- The national Certificate III separates cabling and wireless specialisations and includes hands-on performance requirements. The cabling rules specify which customer work requires registration or direct supervision. Carrier authorisation and each employer’s criteria are additional questions. [Training register ICT30524](https://training.gov.au/training/details/ICT30524) · [ACMA Cabling Provider Rules](https://www.acma.gov.au/cabling-provider-rules) · [ACMA direct supervision](https://www.acma.gov.au/unregistered-cablers-must-be-supervised)
- nbn’s equipment guides show the actual fibre, power and data connections and which lights match each model. VIAVI’s inspection lesson shows how cleanliness is checked on both ends. The exercises add a repeatable test and next-team handover. [nbn FTTP guides](https://www.nbnco.com.au/learn/network-technology/fibre-to-the-premises-explained-fttp/connection-box) · [VIAVI inspection and cleaning video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=udepACqms_w)
- The radio lab records the displayed technology, named metric, conditions and actual service result. The voice lesson separates call setup from media direction using a practical packet analysis case. [Android signal measurements](https://source.android.com/docs/core/connect/signal-strength) · [Rohde & Schwarz walk-test equipment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-UuqxGrPsks) · [SharkFest VoIP workshop](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1c2iI-VN7ZQ)
