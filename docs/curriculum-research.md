# Practical ECE curriculum research

Reviewed 20 November 2026. The [Workbench library](../tutorial/reference/r05-practical-learning-library.html) gives learners the source links. This record explains what the sources do in their lessons, how that informed the two books and what the Australian job evidence can support.

The [practical course comparison and skill map](practical-course-crosswalk.md) includes the follow-up review of TAFE units, university lab syllabuses, and service and commissioning guides. It records what changed across the learning routes and the remaining coverage.

## What established training does

| Source | Structure worth adopting | Workbench application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MIT 6.002 Circuits and Electronics labs](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-002-circuits-and-electronics-spring-2007/pages/labs/) | Pair equipment and setup with electrical predictions and measurements. Use lessons and supporting lab handouts together. | P37 derives the expected current, voltage and pressure first. P51 fits two calibration points and holds another back to test the fit. |
| [Tony Kuphaldt’s Experiments in Electric Circuits](https://www.ibiblio.org/kuphaldt/electricCircuits/Exper/index.html) | Each experiment gives parts, related reading, objectives, a schematic or illustration and instructions. The equipment can be simple enough for a home bench. | The new practical chapters specify what is needed and offer sample data, household materials or a simulator for a first pass when specialised equipment is unavailable. |
| [BACnet Institute](https://thebacnetinstitute.org/courses-overview/) and [Siemens SCE](https://www.siemens.com/en-us/content/sce-educational-institutions/documents/) | Teach the protocol and facility perspective, then break implementation into a process description, I/O, logic and testing. | P38 begins with the point and device identities. P39 uses an I/O table, a time-controlled proof exercise and a restart test. P40 uses an event timeline. |
| [Northwestern Modern Robotics](https://modernrobotics.northwestern.edu/chapters/chapter4/) and [ROS-Industrial CLI workshop](https://ros-industrial.github.io/ros2_i_training/_source/basics/ROS2-CLI.html) | Pair each mathematical idea with a focused worked video. Make learners inspect an interface in simulation and check their result. | P43 uses a cardboard arm and interactive geometry to test joint zero. P44 asks for command and pose evidence from separate simulator endpoints. |
| [OpenCV calibration and stereo documentation](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d9/d0c/group__calib3d.html) and [Edge Impulse deployment example](https://github.com/edgeimpulse/expert-projects/blob/main/computer-vision-projects/smart-cashier-with-fomo-raspberry-pi.md) | Separate geometry and feature extraction from learned detection and deployment. | P45 starts with a fixed camera, a ruler and a real part. P46 checks disparity and invalid pixels. P47 tests a learned model with new capture sessions and records misses, false alarms and latency. |
| [KiCad’s guided first project](https://docs.kicad.org/master/en/getting_started_in_kicad/getting_started_in_kicad.html), [Pololu’s Romi hardware guide](https://www.pololu.com/docs/0J69/3.3) and [FOA’s OTDR workbook](https://www.thefoa.org/tech/ref/quickstart/OTDR.html) | Follow the actual hardware or editor and supply model-specific pictures, pins, settings or outputs. | P42 points to identity and module swaps for a documented low-voltage robot. P48 carries the design through to fabrication checks and a probe plan. P53 compares two different optical test results. |
| [Belimo passive room sensor documentation](https://www.belimo.com/ch/shop/en_GB/p?code=01RT-1L-0), [Raspberry Pi Debug Probe guide](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/microcontrollers/debug-probe.html) and [Festo Didactic training](https://www.festo.com/net/supportportal/files/10131/pneumatictrainingbrochure.pdf) | Identify the exact sensor profile or interface, then show the physical connection before asking the learner to use an instrument, a probe or a simulator. | P54 mounts and verifies a sensor on a mock wall. P55 introduces a known firmware fault and proves which tool can see it. P56 separates the electrical command, air path and actual movement. |
| [Universal Robots 3D handling demo](https://video.universal-robots.com/vision-handling-set-3d-r) and [Adafruit’s testing fixture](https://learn.adafruit.com/how-to-build-a-testing-fixture/overview) | Show how sensors or probes contact a repeatable workpiece, then identify the checks that the rest of the system relies on. | P57 simulates a full inspection cycle with ten cases. P58 checks a board fixture with known passes and failures and records the result. |

Learners should be able to start by pointing at the part or UI control they need, make one measurable prediction, compare it with a real or explicitly simulated reading, deliberately introduce one relevant fault and repeat the test after correction. Position photos and videos at the step where they answer a concrete question. Use range, unit, model and timestamp in the worked evidence. Use stand-alone theory for explanation and link the learner back to the task.

## Job evidence and scope

- The archived **1–2 November 2026** Opportunity Radar snapshot in [C00](../tutorial/career/c00-what-job-ads-ask-for.html) covered 2,764 ads. It recorded 218 instrumentation and PLC matches, 51 BMS and controls matches and 52 electronics technician matches. Their displayed median applicant counts were 28, 19 and 35.5 respectively, compared with 38 in the full snapshot. The search used keywords and categories overlap.
- A trainee title can describe a transition for an already qualified worker. The [Johnson Controls BMS trainee advert](https://jobs.johnsoncontrols.com/job/WD30260794) requires an HVAC qualification and commercial service experience. The [Integrata electrical apprentice listing](https://www.integrata.com.au/contact-1-1-1) describes first- or second-year trade training on controls projects. The [Science Squad advert](https://au.seek.com/job/95569799) describes supervised instrument and robot assembly with test checklists. [Dematic Australia](https://www.dematic.com/en-au/about/careers/apprenticeships/) records electrical and mechanical apprenticeships in warehouse automation. Employer pages can close or move. [Jobs and Skills Australia projections](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/data/employment-projections/occupation) describe occupations at national scale. Local adverts give the requirements of each actual role.
- [C18](../tutorial/career/c18-controls-robotics-and-service-careers.html) ties these observations to portfolio work and local searches for BMS, industrial maintenance, automation service, electronics test, RF and fibre, and vision or robotics integration. It keeps job eligibility and hiring appetite separate from what can be practised on a bench.

## Coverage to develop further

Expand existing lessons where a real measurement or physical sequence is still missing. Further work includes supervised air balancing, deeper electronics production test, more Android modules and laptop thermal service, and additional welding fault measurements on safe trainers. For advanced robot service, develop multi-axis geometry, safe commissioning and industrial camera-to-robot calibration. Keep vendor, simulator and real-equipment exercises clearly identified.
