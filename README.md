# ECE Workbench

A practical electronics course with a shared foundation for repair work, building controls and electronics projects. Start with the [ECE foundation roadmap](tutorial/practice/p00-foundation-roadmap.html), then choose a route once you can predict, measure and explain the bench results.

- **Practice Book** covers the bench, measurement, soldering and diagnostics. It includes Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP32, Modbus, networks and cabling. Each new lab links to the theory behind the parts and readings.
- **Controls, automation and robotics** covers point calibration, BACnet, PLCs, SCADA, motor condition, robot fault-finding, ROS 2, stereo depth and edge vision. [Controls lab](tutorial/practice/p37-current-loop-calibration.html) · [Robot repair](tutorial/practice/p42-mobile-robot-fault-finding.html) · [Machine vision](tutorial/practice/p45-2d-machine-vision.html)
- **Design and test** connects KiCad boards, CAN, sensor calibration, energy logging, VNA measurements and fibre testing. [PCB design and test](tutorial/practice/p48-kicad-testable-pcb.html) · [Practical learning library](tutorial/reference/r05-practical-learning-library.html)
- **Theory Book** covers circuits, device physics, feedback and signals. [T24–T32](tutorial/theory/t24-measurement-datasheets.html) add measurement loading, filters, board rework, startup sequencing, battery charging, USB-C, firmware timing, control and RF.
- **Android repair** starts with the Pixel 4a screen, loudspeaker, headphone jack and battery, then uses a Galaxy A54 to show how to diagnose a charging problem and identify the USB-C daughterboard. [P34 Pixel 4a](tutorial/practice/p34-phone-module-service.html) · [P36 Pixel battery](tutorial/practice/p36-android-battery-service.html) · [P35 Galaxy A54](tutorial/practice/p35-phone-board-triage.html)
- **Laptop repair** starts with the parts of a desktop PC, then follows SSD and RAM replacement on a Framework laptop and the first power checks on a donor. [P32 component and module repair](tutorial/practice/p32-laptop-service.html) · [P33 power diagnostics](tutorial/practice/p33-laptop-power-diagnostics.html)
- **BMS and Australia Job Track** covers BMS, HVAC, fire, CCTV, access control, cabling, NBN, PLCs and licences. Build the [simulated air handler](tutorial/practice/p29-bms-commissioning-lab.html) to practise point checks, fault tests and handover.
- **Embedded and RF projects** include [a timed controller](tutorial/practice/p27-firmware-state-machine.html), [a line-following robot](tutorial/practice/p28-line-following-robot.html), [an IoT reliability lab](tutorial/practice/p30-iot-reliability.html) and [an indoor receive antenna](tutorial/practice/p31-antenna-receive-lab.html).
- **Welding Repair Track** starts at [W00](tutorial/welding/w00-start-here.html). Eight chapters cover machine families, a battery rectifier lab, unpowered diagnosis, a [5 V switching and oscilloscope lab](tutorial/welding/w04-switches-drivers.html#scope-a-pwm-switch), worked faults, parts and return to service.
- **Power-electronics physics**: T20–T23 add IGBTs, switching converters and feedback, fast rectifiers, capacitor ageing and thermal failure. Repair chapters also link to the existing diode, MOSFET, transformer and circuit lessons.
- **Repair work and portfolio** covers intake, quotes, verification and case records in [C16](tutorial/career/c16-repair-work-portfolio.html). The [parts index](tutorial/reference/r03-symbols-parts.html) connects individual parts and symbols to theory lessons and bench work.
- **Job paths and sources** compare Australian roles and practical evidence in [C18](tutorial/career/c18-controls-robotics-and-service-careers.html). The [curriculum research](docs/curriculum-research.md) records how established guides and the SEEK snapshot informed the routes.
- **Practical course comparison** connects TAFE competencies, university labs and service training to the course’s exercises. [Course comparison and skill map](docs/practical-course-crosswalk.md) · [Learner roadmap](tutorial/practice/p00-foundation-roadmap.html)
- **Specialist repair in India** compares services a Ludhiana shop could offer and links practical lessons for plasma cutters, packaging, label printers and cooling equipment. [Shop guide](tutorial/career/c19-ludhiana-repair-shop.html) · [Research notes](docs/india-repair-shop-research.md)
- **Melbourne jobs** compares employer examples, entry requirements and practical work to show when applying. [Local job guide](tutorial/career/c20-ludhiana-melbourne-technician-jobs.html) · [Source notes](docs/ludhiana-melbourne-job-market.md)
- **Telecom work in Melbourne** has six practice sessions for NBN support, fibre cross-connects, network incidents, mobile service, IP phone audio and HFC coax. Follow the job guide to choose a route. [Telecom job guide](tutorial/career/c21-melbourne-telecom-jobs.html) · [Service-path theory](tutorial/theory/t44-telecom-networks.html) · [Research notes](docs/melbourne-telecom-research.md)

- **Commercial controls equipment** covers duct-pressure transmitters, hydronic valve actuators, fan proof, room CO₂, CT power meters, IO-Link and 24 V branch protection. [Hands-on learning route](tutorial/index.html#commercial-devices) · [HVAC device theory](tutorial/theory/t45-commercial-hvac-device-signals.html) · [Metering and field data theory](tutorial/theory/t46-commercial-power-and-device-data.html)

Construction labs use batteries or isolated extra-low-voltage supplies. Actual welding equipment contains hazardous mains-connected circuits and stored energy. Inside-machine work requires a competent repair mentor, model-specific isolation/discharge verification and appropriate service equipment; energised diagnosis and return-to-service checks are supervised technician work. Australian equipment-repair licensing depends on the state and scope.

The welding track includes a bridge-rectifier meter trainer, an illustrative IGBT loss calculator, a PWM timing model and a [fillable, printable repair job sheet](tutorial/reference/r04-welding-job-sheet.html). All run locally with no backend. Job-sheet entries are not saved across page reloads.
