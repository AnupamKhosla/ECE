P33

Laptop board diagnosis and power rails

Learn the order of a no-power investigation on a 5 V trainer, then map the same questions to a documented donor laptop.

Time 90 minBefore this P24, P26, P32

Learn the four checks on one regulator

A rail is a supply connection shared by parts that need the same voltage. A 3.3 V rail can feed the logic for many devices. IN is the voltage presented to the regulator. EN (enable) is a control signal that requests operation. OUT is the voltage it makes. Some regulators also give a power-good signal when the output is in range. A regulator can have input power and a correct zero reading at its output while it waits for EN.

Before measuring, draw a row for source, input after the jumper, standby output, B’s EN and run output. Put the meter in DC volts and reference every measurement to the same ground or supply return. See P03’s voltage probe setup.

Watch on YouTube · Afrotechmods builds a supply with a linear regulator. Locate input, ground and output in the demonstration. The extra EN connection is what you will control in the trainer below.

Build a two-rail trainer

Use an isolated 5 V bench supply, a DMM, a switch, two documented 3.3 V regulator breakout boards with active-high enable pins, a 10 kΩ resistor and a 1 kΩ resistor. Choose breakouts rated for 5 V input, with their required capacitors fitted, that accept 3.3 V on EN and have no permanent enable strap. The TLV755P in P24 is an example of the regulator type. Use the breakout instructions and pin labels. Power off for each wiring change.

  1. Wire input and standby. Connect both module inputs to the 5 V rail through a removable jumper, and connect both grounds to the supply return. Wire regulator A’s EN to its input and label its output STBY.

  2. Wire the run command. Put the 10 kΩ resistor from regulator B’s EN to ground. Connect STBY to B’s EN through the switch. Label B’s output RUN and attach 1 kΩ from RUN to return as its load.

  3. Capture both states. With a low current limit suited to the two modules and the load, measure source, after-jumper input, STBY, B’s EN and RUN against return. With input connected, the first two nodes are about 5 V and STBY about 3.3 V. With the switch open, EN and RUN settle near zero. With it closed, EN and RUN are about 3.3 V. The load draws about 3.3 mA.

Turn off power before removing the input jumper. Bring the current-limited trainer back up and measure each node. The same zero at RUN can mean the regulator received no request or that supply never reached it. Put the jumper back with power off and prove both rails again. Replacing regulator B would leave either fault in place.

Transfer the sequence to a donor laptop

Watch on YouTube · RTC, 3V & 5V Are OK But Laptop Still Won't Turn ON? | What to Check Next? | Full Logic in Course 7.0 · Laptex
Follow the reasoning after some standby rails are present. Write which evidence shows the next startup condition is missing.

Start with P32’s external checks and confirm the exact board revision and documented test points. Work on a spare with no data to preserve. Follow the manual’s isolation procedure before opening or taking resistance or diode readings. Inspect connectors, flexes and components under magnification. Locate the input connector, input protection, charge circuit, standby rails and enable or power-good signals from approved documentation. Use a documented return reference. Use powered measurements on the trainer first. On a donor, only use model-documented, accessible low-voltage points and an equipped mentor when the procedure calls for an open powered board.

Documented pointQuestion it answersWhat comes next
Input before and after protectionDoes supported adapter power reach the downstream converter?Check a transition across the connector, fuse or input switch. A blown fuse is a symptom. Identify the reason before replacement.
Standby rail and power requestCan the startup logic receive the power-button event?Verify power-button flex and documented prerequisite signals before blaming the controller.
Run regulator IN, EN, OUT and PGIs the stage requested, powered and confirming its output?Use the first missing prerequisite to choose the next test. A rail expected only after sequencing can correctly be off in standby.

Record a cold resistance or diode-mode reading with polarity, reference and setting, and compare against the same test on an identical board revision if available. A low reading alone does not establish a short or identify a part. Keep first board-level exercises to measured localization, and refer unknown rails, extensive liquid damage or data-critical devices for specialist work.
