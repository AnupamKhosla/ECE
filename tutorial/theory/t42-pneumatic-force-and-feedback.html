T42

Pneumatic force, directional valves and independent feedback

A PLC output can command air to move a load. The final motion depends on pressure, flow, mechanical constraints and measured position.

Time 25 minutesBefore this T03 · T18 · T31

Pressure and force are separate from flow and speed

For a piston with a pressure difference Δp across effective area A, ideal force is Δp × A. For a 25 mm bore, piston area is about 491 mm². At 0.30 MPa difference the ideal cap-end force is about 147 N. Return force differs because the rod reduces area on one side. Real force is affected by friction, pressure drop and exhaust conditions.

Speed comes from how quickly air moves and pressure builds under load. A high pressure indication at the supply does not prove adequate flow through the valve or tubing during a stroke.

Read positions and independent sensors

A 5/2 directional valve names five ports and two positions. Palm or solenoid actuation is distinct from the return method, which may use a spring or another operator. A mid-position or emergency response depends on the actual design. Work from the complete symbol and supporting instructions.

The controller can see a command and two limits. If both incompatible limits are true, record invalid feedback before accepting the position. If neither is true in the middle of travel, interpret it against travel timing and the specified sensing arrangement.

Understand what remains after electrical power drops

Air can remain trapped in sections of the circuit. Mechanical loads can also retain energy through gravity or springs. Switching off the electrical command alone leaves those sources to address. Real systems require a documented isolation and validation procedure suitable for their specific mechanism. Festo’s training packages show how to build up from basic symbols and components.
