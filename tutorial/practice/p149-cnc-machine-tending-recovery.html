P149

Recover a CNC tending cycle with a part between the gripper and chuck

An interrupted transfer can leave a part in the gripper, in the chuck or held by both. A controller restart cannot tell you which one happened. Recover from physical evidence.

Time 70–95 minutesBefore this P144 · P146 · P147

Work with a part and two holders

Draw a door, spindle chuck and robot gripper on paper or use two binder clips and a paper part on a table. Label the part 42 and designate separate robot and CNC controllers. This exercise rehearses ordinary sequencing with no machine connected. The actual installed application has a separate engineered and validated safety system for hazardous motion, access and energy. Retreat and hand overChuckRobot outside the loading volume, door in its required state, correct part and recipe still confirmed. CNC retains its own cycle-start conditions.

Before changing ownership, record the job ID, part location, fresh observations and both controllers’ phases. A process flag saying the robot is outside helps sequencing. Its use for protecting people or controlling a hazardous machine function requires the safety function specified in the design and a separate validation record.

Interrupt three handoffs

  1. Empty chuck closure. The close request was sent and the chuck closed signal is on, but seating proof is missing. Name the last confirmed holder and which two actions must wait.
  2. Restart during shared hold. Part 42 is still between both holders. The PLC restarts and the CNC reports clamp pressure but its part record is unavailable. Decide which record and observed evidence must agree before release or retreat.
  3. Stale robot clearance. The chuck has the part, but the robot-clear message stopped before the last movement. The machine control must keep its next step held. Explain which current source would prove actual clearance in the approved design.
Check the recovery decisions

In the first case the gripper remains the last confirmed holder. Hold release and machining. In the second case record the shared or uncertain hold and use the approved recovery procedure. Identify job 42, establish its physical seating and prove both holder states before the responsible team selects a recovery. In the third case a retained clearance flag is stale. Verify current position and the independent condition required by the design before advancing.

Repeat the exercise for unloading. Establish grip before relinquishing the chuck, then verify that the part left and the chuck and robot are ready for the next job. For handover keep the job history, first fault, record reconciliation, observed holder proof and signed outcome. Universal Robots · Example mapping between a robot and PLC.
